How We Use Your Personal Information

Depending on the Activision websites, products and services (“Activision Properties”) you interact with, we may use your information to:
  • provide you with our games and their features
  • provide you with and tailor your Activision account, online forums and their features
  • enable you to interact with other Activision Properties
  • send newsletters periodically by email about our products and services which you have opted into receiving
  • run contests or promotions on Activision Properties and provide winners with prizes
  • debug and improve our current and future games
  • send you surveys in order to improve player experience with our games
  • offer you the ability to email or message friends or family by using our "send-a-friend" tool
  • provide you with online advertising selected by Activision
  • provide you with customer service and assist you with resolving any issues you may have
  • provide a fair gaming and community experience, for example by using anti-fraud technologies such as bans or blocks of accounts
  • enable you to interact with Activision Properties though social networking sites and use the information from these interactions, solely in accordance with your Social Media privacy settings, to:
    • tailor content, marketing, and improve and adjust user experience relating to Activision Properties;
    • give you exclusive content;
    • personalize your online experience with us within and outside our Properties; and
    • contact you in accordance with applicable marketing preferences through Social Media or directly by sending you the latest news, special offerings, and rewards.
Please see the Activision Privacy Policy at Activision Privacy Policy [and the Activision Cookie Policy] for further information.

Information We Collect

The information we collect from you can be broken down into a number of categories:
  • Data from your interactions with our games

    o You can access the data your share from your Activision account at any time by logging into your account and reviewing your profile settings.
  • Data from your interactions on our websites
    o For example: forum posts and IP address
  • Data from your interactions with Activision Properties through social networking sites
    o We encourage you to check the privacy settings of your social networking accounts to review what data is shared with third parties you interact with
  • Customer service data
    o For example: customer support chat records and issue descriptions.

Who We Send Your Data To

As set out in detail in our Privacy Policy, Activision shares Information:
  • with the various Activision group companies for the purposes described in our Privacy Policy;
  • with vendors and partners engaged by Activision in order to provide you with goods or services you have requested or online advertising selected by us;
  • if required to do so by applicable law, regulation or legal process;
  • if necessary to exercise, establish or defend our legal rights;
  • if necessary to protect the vital interests of any person; and
  • in connection with the sale of our business or assets.
Please note that any information, including personal information that you reveal in a bulletin board, message board, chat room or other public forum is publicly viewable. If you would like to request your Activision Forum data, please log in to your forum account and access your data directly from your account. Also note that your personal data is transferred and processed in the United States where many of our central databases operate.

Who Your Data Comes From

In some instances, Activision receives data about you from other sources. These sources include:
  • Platform providers from which you access Activision properties, including: Playstation (Sony), Xbox (Microsoft), Nintendo, Steam, Apple, Google, Battle.net (Blizzard)
  • Social networking sites through which you interact with Activision Properties: Facebook, Twitch, Twitter, Snapchat, Instagram

How Long We Keep Your Data

We delete your personal information which we process for the performance of the contract or based on your consent in case you terminate your contract with us, close your Activision account, withdraw your consent or where you request us to delete your personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. As an exception, we will not delete your personal information where we still have a legitimate interest to use your personal information. In any case, we will not retain or use your personal information longer than necessary for the purposes outlined in our Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

You have the rights to correct, update, or request deletion of your personal information set out in the report and in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

You may object to the processing of your personal information based on legitimate interest on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may control the extent to which we market to you and you have the right to request that we stop sending you marketing messages at any time.

Where we rely on your consent in order to process your personal information, you have the right to withdraw such consent to further use of your personal information at any time.

We hope that we can satisfy queries you may have about the way we process your personal information. However, if you have unresolved concerns you also have the right to complain to data protection authorities. You can bring the complaint in your member state of residence, place of work or where an alleged infringement of data protection law occurred.
